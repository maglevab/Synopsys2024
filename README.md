Modernization of Electrical Grid: Innovative Device & Real-Time Data Analytic Software for Detecting Fallen Power Lines

Every year, torrential storms, fallen power poles and power lines wreak havoc on the community and severely affect the electrical infrastructure. The utility companies currently rely on slow, expensive, and inefficient methods to detect fallen power poles. My solution, PowerPulse (low-cost device + monitoring software + web interface) includes a device attached to power poles that can quickly detect fallen power poles and cut live wire. The device consists of an altitude sensor to detect the fallen pole and a light sensor to detect the power flashes of a cut wire. Cut live wires are the hardest for utility companies to detect, and pose the greatest danger. The device relays the data to pre-programmed servers and the utility worker web interface displays the criticality status of each pole within one second of the pole falling. The server calculates criticality of the pole using a scoring algorithm based on various factors: population around the pole, proximity to substation, and zone (urban/forest). Higher levels of criticality correspond to different colors on the interface making it easy for the worker to identify the most critical pole. The device was 90% accurate in detecting fallen poles and 100% accurate in detecting cut wires. The criticality algorithm was simulated on 6000+ poles, achieving 100% accuracy in calculating the criticality of the pole. I conducted community outreach by talking to Silicon Valley Power and received valuable feedback. By swiftly identifying and prioritizing critical poles to fix, PowerPulse mitigates risks, enhances safety, and minimizes disruptions for entire communities.
